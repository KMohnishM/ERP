About College ERP System

A comprehensive solution for educational institution management

System Overview

This College ERP System is designed to unify data from various departments including admissions, fee collection, hostel allocation, and examination records. The system provides a centralized platform for managing all aspects of college administration efficiently.

Key Modules

Admissions

Manage student applications, enrollment, and course registrations.

Fee Management

Handle fee collections, payment schedules, and financial records.

Hostel Management

Manage room allocations, hostel facilities, and student accommodations.

Examination System

Handle exam scheduling, results processing, and grade management.
